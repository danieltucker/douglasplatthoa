Getting Started

How to set up your owner account, make payments, and access community documents through Windermere's portal.

🚀 Setting Up Your Owner Portal

1

Contact Windermere to Get Set Up

Call Windermere Management at (360) 733-7944 or email brandye@windermeremgt.com to get your owner account created in AppFolio.

Have your property address ready. They'll set up your account and send you an invitation to the portal.

2

Accept Your Portal Invitation

Look for an email invitation from AppFolio. Follow the link to create your password and activate your account. Check your spam folder if you don't see it within a day or two.

3

Log In to the Owner Portal

Once your account is active, you can log in any time at the link below to make payments, view your account balance, access documents, and update your contact information.
